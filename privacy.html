PawLog

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 July 2026 · Last updated 29 July 2026

PawLog does not collect your data. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you log about your pet is stored in a database on your own phone and stays there.

Who this policy is from

PawLog is an app made and published by Petros Chol, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use PawLog on your Android or iOS device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive, see, store, or have any way to access the information you enter into PawLog. That includes:

  • your pet's name, species, breed, birthday, weight, and conditions
  • symptoms, medications, doses, meals, water, and notes you log
  • photos you attach to a diary entry
  • reminder times, settings, and anything else in the app

All of it is written to a private database inside PawLog's own storage area on your device. The app makes no network request to any server of ours, because we do not run one.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications — so medication reminders can appear at the times you set. Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely by your phone. Nothing is sent through a server, and we never learn whether a reminder fired or was tapped.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android) — so a reminder arrives at the exact minute you chose instead of drifting. Used only for your own reminders.
  • Photos — asked for only at the moment you choose to attach a photo to an entry. PawLog reads the single image you pick and copies it into its own storage. It does not browse, scan, or upload your photo library.

Files you create yourself

PawLog can build two kinds of file for you, both produced on your device:

  • a PDF report for your vet
  • a full backup (a .zip containing your entries and photos)

These are never sent anywhere by the app. They exist on your phone until you choose to do something with them. If you then share one — by email, by saving it to a cloud drive, by handing it to your vet — that sharing is your action, through whatever app you picked, and it is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours.

Purchases (PawLog Plus)

PawLog is free to use, and logging is never restricted. An optional upgrade called PawLog Plus unlocks extra features. When PawLog Plus is available for purchase, buying it is handled by your app store (Google Play or Apple), with purchase and subscription status managed through RevenueCat, Inc.

This is the only situation in which PawLog communicates over the internet, and the only thing that travels is the purchase itself — whether this device owns the upgrade. No pet data, no health information, and no diary content is ever involved in, or visible to, that check. Your app store and RevenueCat handle payment data under their own policies: Google, Apple, RevenueCat.

Your purchase status is remembered on the device so that Plus keeps working when you are offline.

Sharing with third parties

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. We do not sell data. We do not transfer data. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, crash reporting services, or software development kits collecting information inside PawLog. If your app store shows you aggregate statistics about downloads or crashes, those are collected by the store from users who opted into sharing with the store — not by PawLog.

Deleting your information

Because your information lives only on your phone, you are always in control of it:

  • Delete a single log entry, a photo, or an entire pet from inside the app.
  • Uninstall PawLog to remove the app and its database from the device completely.

There is no server-side copy for us to delete, and no request you need to send us. We would like you to export a backup first if you may want the diary later, because once it is deleted from the phone it cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Children

PawLog is a tool for pet owners and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. The promise at the top — that your pet's diary stays on your phone — is a core commitment of the product and is not something we intend to weaken.
